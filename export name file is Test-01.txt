The Little Star

Once upon a time, there was a little star named Sparkle. She lived in the night sky alongside millions of other stars. Every evening, she would shine as brightly as she could, hoping to stand out from the rest.

One night, a young girl named Lily looked up at the sky and whispered, "I wish I could be as bright as that little star." Sparkle heard her wish and decided to help.

From that night on, Sparkle shone brighter than ever before, casting a warm golden glow all the way down to Earth. Lily would look up every evening and smile, feeling brave and hopeful.

Years passed, and Lily grew up to become an astronomer. She spent her life studying the stars, always remembering the little star that gave her courage when she needed it most.

And Sparkle? She continued to shine brightly, knowing that even the smallest light can make a big difference.

The End.
